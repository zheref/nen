- `nen issue file` and `nen idea file` take `--requirements-from <catalogue.json> [--kind <kind>]` (zheref/nen#476's scope, "the same check before the create"): the body is held to the issue-sections catalogue by the same function `issue body-check` runs, on the bytes about to be sent, and any miss refuses the filing at exit 1 before any GitHub read (`body-check (kind '<kind>'): <miss> …; nothing was filed`; under `--json` the refusal envelope). Advisories never block. `--kind` without `--requirements-from`, an unreadable or malformed catalogue, and an undeclared kind exit 2. Without the flag the filings are unchanged.
