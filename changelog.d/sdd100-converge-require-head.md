- `nen pr converge --require-head <sha>` pins the verdict to one head (zheref/nen#483 AC-6): the pull request's first read must be at the pin, and a head-only re-read after every closing issue was read must still be; a first read at another head, a head that moved between the reads, or a failed re-read exits 1 with stdout empty, naming both heads. A pin that is not a full SHA exits 2 before any read. Without the flag the verb reads as before.
