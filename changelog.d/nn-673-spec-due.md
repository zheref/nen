- `nen spec due --repo <spec> [--family <name>] [--json]` (contract `nen.spec.due/v0.1`) reports, for every family the spec repository's `nen/repos.json` declares under `families[]`, whether it is due a recapture. Each member's newest `v<major>.<minor>.<patch>` tag (prereleases and other `v*` spellings are no release) is held against the commit its `capture/<CODE>/inventory.json` records, and GitHub's compare decides whether the tag is newer. Each family is `due` (naming the member, its newest tag and the recorded commit), `current`, `no-capture` or `not-decided` (a member's tags, compare or capture entry could not be read, or the capture records another repository's origin), never current by default. Exit 0 only when every family is current.
- The verb is read-only: every GitHub call is a `gh api` GET, it writes nothing, and it starts no recapture.
