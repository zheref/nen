- `nen review ledger record --persona <id> --outcome delta --from-head <sha> --to-head <sha>` records hanten's bounded delta pass: the completed re-read of a spent reviewer's own earlier review on a new head. It appends `{ at, outcome: "delta", fromHead, toHead }` and leaves `used` and `max` unchanged, so an exhausted budget no longer forces a false `skipped-exhausted` row or a reset. It is refused at exit 2, before anything is written and naming the bad input, for a missing head, a head given with another outcome, a head that is not a full 40-digit lowercase commit id, equal heads, a reviewer with no `ran` pass or with budget left, a duplicate pair anywhere in the cycle, and, among the deltas since the reviewer's last `ran` pass, a `--from-head` other than the last delta's head or a `--to-head` those deltas already reviewed; a later `ran` resets that chain.
- `decide`'s `reviewers[]` and `record`'s report now carry per-reviewer `deltas` (the cycle's total), `skips` and `lastDeltaHead` (the last delta's head since the last `ran`, else `null`), so a round (`used`), a skip and a delta are told apart without parsing rows. The reader accepts a `delta` row only in that shape and still requires `used` to equal the `ran` rows; a malformed stored delta is refused as inconsistent history, never repaired. Legacy ledgers read unchanged and the contract stays `hatsu.hanten.cycle/v0.1`.
- A nen older than this change refuses a ledger holding a `delta` row as inconsistent history, so a caller records deltas only after its nen pin moves to a release that carries them.
