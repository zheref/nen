`nen direct registry --json` preserves each alias's declared `line`. New `nen wc prune` validates a closed explicit plan and removes only named merged remote branches or stale tracking refs, with exact endpoint/OID guards and truthful per-item results. Tracking rows explicitly name their configured remote and reverse exact/single-wildcard fetch mappings; excluded/ambiguous mappings and duplicate destructive targets refuse. Worktree and local-branch removals are unsupported and refuse in both preview and execution, preserving files, registrations, refs and routing configuration; safe writer/checkout serialization remains outstanding (refs #409). Host-link rows are readlink-only. No global prune or object GC runs, and uncertain writes never claim freed bytes.
